# Alien in the Backyard

A spaceship crash-lands in Kellan and Diana's backyard. Out hops George, a friendly (original!) silver-suited furry alien, and Bernie the dog sniffs out the story: six shiny ship pieces fell off near the school. Walk from home, down the lane and the court, along the main road to the school, then search leaves, balls, holes, bushes, bins and more in the front and back playgrounds. Bernie's nose meter (cold → hot) and glints help. Bring all six pieces back to George and watch him fix his ship and fly home.

Single-file HTML5 game — open `index.html`. Keyboard: arrows to walk, Space to search. On touch screens use the on-screen buttons. Pick Kellan or Diana on the title screen. Progress is saved automatically (Continue on the title screen).

Street names are generic; layout is only loosely modelled on the neighborhood.

## Tests
`python3 tests/run.py` — reachability of every search spot, a full scripted playthrough, save, and real-time keyboard check. `python3 tests/shots.py` — screenshots.
